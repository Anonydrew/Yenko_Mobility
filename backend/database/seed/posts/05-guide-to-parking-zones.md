Picture a student in a wheelchair arriving at the ramp outside their faculty building, only to find it blocked by an e-bike. It takes just one badly parked bike to ruin someone's day. That's why every Yenko ride has to end inside a parking zone.

## How parking zones work

Parking zones show up in the app as blue areas with a **P** icon. On the ground, most zones also have painted lines or a Yenko sign. We put them where students actually need them, such as outside lecture halls, libraries, halls of residence, main gates and food courts, and we agree every location with the university.

There are now more than **120 parking zones** across our campuses, and we add new ones every month based on where you ride.

## Ending your ride, step by step

1. **Ride into a blue zone.** Your phone vibrates once you're inside.
2. **Park the bike upright** on its kickstand, facing the same way as any other bikes.
3. **Close the lock.** Push the lever on the rear wheel down until it clicks.
4. **Take a photo.** The app asks for a quick photo so we can see the bike is parked well.
5. **Check the summary.** You'll see the ride time and cost straight away.

If the lock won't close, you're probably just outside the zone. Move a few metres further in and try again.

## Never park

- on wheelchair ramps, tactile paving or stairs,
- in front of doors, gates or fire exits,
- in the middle of walkways, or
- on grass or flower beds.

## What if I can't reach a zone?

If a zone is full, the app points you to the nearest zone with space, which is usually less than two minutes away. If you really can't get to one, for example because of a flat tyre, contact support in the app and we'll end your ride remotely.

If a ride ends outside a zone without a good reason, we add a **GH₵ 10 out-of-zone fee**. It isn't there to make money. The fee pays for a field team member to collect the bike and return it to a zone. Riders who repeatedly block ramps or exits may have their accounts suspended.

## Help us keep campuses clear

If you spot a Yenko bike parked where it shouldn't be, tap **Report a bike** in the app and add a photo. We'll usually move it within the hour, and you'll get 10 free minutes of riding as a thank-you.
