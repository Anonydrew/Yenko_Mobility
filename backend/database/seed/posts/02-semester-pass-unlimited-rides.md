Since we launched, the most common request in our inbox has been some version of: *"I ride every day. Can I just pay once?"*

Now you can. The **Yenko Semester Pass** gives you unlimited rides for the whole semester at one flat price.

## How it compares

| | Pay as you go | Day Pass | Semester Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | GH₵ 2 to unlock + GH₵ 0.50/min | GH₵ 25 | GH₵ 250 |
| Rides included | Pay per ride | Unlimited for 24 hours | Unlimited all semester |
| Ride length | No limit | Up to 45 min per ride | Up to 45 min per ride |
| Extra minutes | – | GH₵ 0.30/min | GH₵ 0.30/min |

Each ride on the pass can last up to 45 minutes, which is more than enough for almost any trip on campus. Our average ride is just nine minutes. If you do go over, you only pay for the extra minutes.

## Who it's for

If you ride **four or more times a week**, the Semester Pass pays for itself within the first month. It's a great fit if you:

- live off campus and ride to lectures every day,
- move between faculties several times a day, or
- work a part-time job or internship near campus.

If you only ride now and then, pay as you go is still the cheaper choice. The app will tell you which option suits you best based on your last 30 days of rides.

## Paying for it

Buy the pass in the app under **Wallet → Passes**. You can pay with MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money or a debit card.

Prefer not to pay GH₵ 250 at once? Choose **Split payment** to pay GH₵ 125 now and the rest six weeks later. There are no fees and no interest.

## Student verification

The Semester Pass is for enrolled students. To verify, add your university email address in the app. We'll send you a six-digit code, and the whole thing takes less than a minute. Staff passes are coming later this year.

## Common questions

**Can I pause my pass?** Yes. If you're away for more than two weeks, for example on an internship or medical leave, contact support and we'll extend your pass by the time you missed.

**Does it work on every campus?** Yes. Your pass works at every Yenko campus, including on inter-campus visits and exchange trips.

**What happens at the end of the semester?** Your pass expires on the date shown in the app. We'll remind you a week before, and nothing renews automatically.
