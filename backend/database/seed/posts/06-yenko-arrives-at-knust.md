**Akwaaba, KNUST!** As of this week, 150 Yenko E1 e-bikes are live on the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology campus in Kumasi, our second and largest campus yet.

## Why KNUST

The KNUST campus covers more than 16 square kilometres. For many students, the walk from their hostel to lectures takes 25 minutes each way, often in full sun. Shuttle buses help, but at peak times the queue can take longer than the journey.

When we surveyed students before launch, **7 in 10** said getting around campus was one of the biggest daily frustrations of university life. More than 2,000 people joined our waitlist in the first week.

## Where to find bikes

We worked with the university and the Students' Representative Council to map out **24 parking zones** where students need them most, including:

- Commercial Area and the Ayeduase Gate,
- the halls of residence, including Unity, Republic and Queen Elizabeth II Hall,
- the College of Engineering and the KNUST School of Business,
- the main library and the Great Hall, and
- the private hostels around Brunei.

You'll find the full map in the app and a list of zones on the KNUST campus page of our website.

## Launch offer

To celebrate, **your first three rides at KNUST are free** (up to 30 minutes each) until the end of the month. You don't need a code. The free rides are added to your account automatically once you verify your student email.

## A local team

Yenko KNUST is run by a team of 18 people from Kumasi, including field technicians, battery swappers and support agents, many of them KNUST graduates. Our new campus hub is open every day from 6 am to 10 pm. Stop by to pick up a free helmet with your Semester Pass, report a problem or just say hello.

## Become a Campus Ambassador

We're looking for 10 KNUST students to join our Campus Ambassador programme. Ambassadors help new riders get started, share feedback from their faculties, and earn free rides plus a monthly stipend. Applications are open now on our Careers page.
