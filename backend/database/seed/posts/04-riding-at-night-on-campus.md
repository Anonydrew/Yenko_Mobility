Almost a third of Yenko rides start after 6 pm. Late library sessions, evening lectures, choir rehearsals and hall week events keep campuses busy long after dark. Riding at night is perfectly safe with the right habits, but it asks a little more of you.

## Be seen, not just lit

Every Yenko bike has front and rear lights that switch on automatically. Lights alone aren't always enough, though, especially on roads with lots of headlight glare.

- **Wear something light or reflective.** A white T-shirt or a reflective band on your arm makes you far easier to spot from the side.
- **Keep the lights clear.** If a bag strap or basket load covers the front light, rearrange it before you set off.
- **Use your bell early.** At night, pedestrians hear you before they see you.

## Choose well-lit routes

Turn on the **Well-lit routes** layer in the app's map settings. It highlights paths with working street lighting, which our campus teams check every month. After sunset, the app suggests these routes by default, even if they add a minute or two.

Some shortcuts that are fine during the day, like unpaved paths behind halls or across sports fields, are closed to rides at night. They show as grey on the map from 7 pm to 6 am.

## Ride a little slower

Potholes, speed bumps and uneven drainage covers are much harder to see at night. Keep your speed down, especially on roads you don't ride every day, and leave extra space between you and the vehicle in front.

## Use the safety tools in the app

- **Share my ride.** Send a friend a live link to your trip. They'll see your route and get a notification when your ride ends.
- **Emergency button.** Press and hold the shield icon for two seconds to call campus security or emergency services. Your GPS location is shared automatically.
- **24/7 support.** Our support line is open all night. If a bike stops working mid-ride, we'll help you end the trip, and you won't be charged for the time.

## Check your campus's hours

Operating hours vary by campus, depending on what we've agreed with each university. You'll find the hours for your campus on its page on our website and in the app. If you're running late, you can always finish a ride that's already in progress.

Night rides are part of campus life, so let's keep them safe.
