Last Tuesday at 7:42 am, a second-year nursing student unlocked a Yenko bike outside Volta Hall at the University of Ghana and rode off to an 8 am lecture. It was an ordinary ride, and it was also our **100,000th**.

We want to share what those rides added up to, and to be open about how we calculated the numbers.

## The numbers

- **100,000** rides
- **231,000 km** travelled, or about 5.8 times around the Earth
- **2.3 km** average ride distance
- **9 minutes** average ride time
- **7:40 am** busiest minute of the week (on Monday mornings, of course)
- **14,200** people who have taken at least one ride

## CO₂ avoided: how we worked it out

It's tempting to assume every e-bike ride replaces a car journey and claim a big number. That wouldn't be honest, because many of our rides replace walking, which produces no emissions at all.

So we asked. In our latest rider survey (1,850 responses), **45%** of riders said that without Yenko they would have made their most recent trip by taxi, ride-hailing app or campus shuttle. The rest would have walked or not made the trip at all.

Here's the calculation:

1. **Distance that replaced a motor vehicle:** 231,000 km × 45% = 103,950 km
2. **Emissions avoided:** 103,950 km × 0.17 kg CO₂ per km (a typical petrol car) ≈ **17.7 tonnes**
3. **Minus our own charging emissions:** about 2,300 kWh of electricity × 0.45 kg CO₂ per kWh ≈ 1.0 tonne
4. **Net CO₂ avoided: about 16.7 tonnes**

It's not a giant number yet, but it's a real one, and it grows every time a rider skips the taxi queue. We haven't yet included the emissions from building the bikes and moving them around. We're working with an independent partner on a full life-cycle assessment and will publish it next year.

## Time and money saved

For riders who would otherwise have walked, the average Yenko trip saves about 18 minutes. Across all those rides, that's more than **16,000 hours** that students got back for studying, sleeping or simply being on time.

Semester Pass holders who used to take taxis save an average of **GH₵ 160 a month**.

## What's next

- **Solar charging** will expand from our Legon hub to KNUST and UCC by early next year.
- **A second life for batteries:** batteries that can no longer power a bike will store solar energy for community lighting projects.
- **A public impact dashboard** on our website, updated every month.

Thank you to everyone who was part of our first 100,000 rides. Here's to the next million.
