The same list is shown publicly at `/credits` (linked in the footer), because CC BY and CC BY-SA licences require visible attribution. The data lives in `frontend/src/content/credits.ts`. **If you replace a photo, update that file.**

All photos were resized and some were cropped to fit the layout.
